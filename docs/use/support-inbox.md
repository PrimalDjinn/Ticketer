# Answer support chats

This page explains how support chats work: how people outside the team start one, and how the team answers them from
the shared inbox, assigns them, writes internal notes, closes them, and turns them into tickets.

## How customers start a chat

People outside the team, including visitors without an account, can start a chat from:

- **Start a chat** on the public help page at `/`, or under **Need help without signing in?** on the sign-in page;
- the chat button in the corner of the public and sign-in pages, labelled **Chat with support**;
- the chat button in the top bar of the app.

They can add a **Subject** and send their first message. A visitor gets a guest session with the first message and
keeps the conversation if they register later.

## Reply time shown to customers

The public help page and the chat panel tell customers how quickly the team usually replies, for example "Usually
replies in about 20 minutes". Ticketer measures it as the median time from the start of a support conversation to the
team's first reply that isn't an internal note, over conversations started in the last 30 days that got a reply.

With fewer than 5 such replies, Ticketer doesn't show a time. The help page then says "The team replies here as soon as
they can", and the chat panel says "The team replies as soon as they can". The figure can be up to a minute old.

Customers see their own conversations only. They see the team's replies, marked **Team**, but never internal notes.

People on the team talk to each other in direct messages instead, under the **Team** tab of the same chat panel.

## Open the inbox

The team finds all conversations under **Conversations** in the sidebar. The badge shows unread or open conversations.
New conversations notify admins.

The inbox has four tabs: **Open**, **Mine** (assigned to you), **Unassigned** and **Closed**. Use the search box to
search by name, email, subject or message text.

Viewers can read conversations but can't reply, assign or close them.

## Reply or write an internal note

1. Select a conversation.
2. The message box starts on **Internal note**, which only the team sees. To answer the customer, select **Reply**: the
   box turns amber and says the customer will see it.
3. Write the message and select **Send** (or **Add note**).

The customer is notified of replies. Internal notes are for the team only; the assignee is notified of them. After
each message the box goes back to **Internal note**, so select **Reply** again for every message the customer should
see.

## Assign a conversation

Choose a person under **Assignee**, or select **Assign to me**. Conversations can be assigned to admins and members.
Replying doesn't assign the conversation to you; assignment is always explicit. The assignee is notified of new
customer messages.

## Close and reopen

Select **Close** when the conversation is done, and **Reopen** to open it again.

- When the customer writes to a closed conversation, it reopens.
- When a teammate replies to a closed conversation, it stays closed.

## Turn a conversation into a ticket

Select **Create ticket**. Ticketer:

1. Opens a private ticket on the customer's behalf. Its title is the subject or the first message, and its
   description is the customer's messages. The conversation's assignee becomes the ticket's assignee.
2. Makes the customer follow the ticket.
3. Posts a reply in the conversation telling the customer the ticket's reference, and notifies them.

After that, the button reads **View ticket**, and the ticket shows **Opened from a support conversation** with a link
back.
